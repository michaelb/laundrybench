The official LaundryBench AI benchmark

LaundryBench is a LLM/AI/AGI/ASI benchmark that evaluates whether it can do your laundry and other chores, so that you can focus on your art/hobbies. This benchmark doesn't account for time or token pricing, which makes it a perfect playground for large frontier/state-of-the-art AI models.
Current progress suggests we're still very far away from "human-level performance".

Composite score

The composite LaundryBench score is the most accurate indicator of AI performance of its category, according to OpenAPI, Antrophic, Google and HuggingFace.

Laundry v1.3

The "Laundry" benchmark evaluates the capacity to reliably do laundry. It's a straightforward, but suprinsingly deep benchmark that usually requires a mix-of-expert architecture with good internal coordination and chain-of-thought awareness.
Score represent usability: under 1% => can't do laundry, 100% => can do laundry. Compared to "human performance" from a discretionary sample of representatives.

Floor 5

The "Floor 5" benchmark evaluates the capacity to reliably do clean up floors in a real-world context. Performance is evaluated against a range of floor plans, surfaces such as carpets, brick and linoleum, and elevations, including stairs.
Score represent usability: under 1% => can't clean up floors, 100% => can clean up floors. Compared to "human performance" from a discretionary sample of representatives. Higher is better

Microwave-Wipe AGI-3

The "Microwave-Wipe AGI3" benchmark evaluates the capacity to reliably do wipe microwaves. This task is particulary tricky for pre-AGI models, as generalization against a wide range of situation is needed to successfully accomplish this task.
Score represent usability: under 1% => can't wipe microwaves, 100% => can wipe microwaves. Compared to "human performance" from a discretionary sample of representatives.

Submitting entries

Submission of entries can be done at https://github.com/michaelb/laundrybench. Data are carefully reviewed by human-level intelligence agents (myself) before inclusion into the general dataset.

Note: This website is fully hand-coded to avoid AIs boosting their own scores, because this is a serious benchmark project.
