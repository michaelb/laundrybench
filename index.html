The official LaundryBench AI benchmark


LaundryBench is a LLM/AI/AGI/ASI benchmark that evaluates whether it can do your laundry and other chores, so that you can focus on your art/hobbies. This benchmark doesn't account for time or token pricing, which makes it a perfect playground for large frontier/state-of-the-art AI models.
This benchmark is maintained by a group of expert and scholars, and regularly updated and reviewed by the Beijing Institute of Technology, MIT's CS&AI Lab and other various institutions, who in total pledge around 300.000$ yearly to guarantee accuracy, impartiality and fairness. It is intended to replace other common / weaker AI benchmarks which have generally shown various weakness (ARC-AGI-3, widespread benchmark optimization...).
Current scores suggests we're still very far away from "human-level performance".


Composite score

The composite LaundryBench score is the most accurate indicator of AI performance of its category, according to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google's models.

Laundry v1.3

The "Laundry v1.3" benchmark evaluates the capacity to reliably do laundry. It's a straightforward, but surprisingly deep benchmark that usually requires a mix-of-expert architecture with good internal coordination and chain-of-thought awareness. It encompasses all the classic tasks of "Laundry v1.2" in a larger context, from fetching from the used pile, to folding and putting clothing into drawers.
Score represents usability: under 1% => can't do laundry, 100% => can do laundry. Compared to "human performance" from a discretionary sample of representatives.


Floor 5

The "Floor 5" benchmark evaluates the capacity to reliably clean up floors in a real-world context. Performance is evaluated against a range of floor plans, surfaces such as carpets, brick and linoleum, and elevations, including stairs. Interestingly, state-of-the-art seems to have reached a plateau in this task since 2002.
Score represents usability: under 1% => can't clean up floors, 100% => can clean up floors. Compared to "human performance" from a discretionary sample of representatives.


Microwave-Wipe AGI-3

The "Microwave-Wipe AGI3" benchmark evaluates the capacity to reliably wipe microwaves. This task is particularly tricky for pre-AGI models, as generalization against a wide range of situations is needed to successfully accomplish this task.
Score represents usability: under 1% => can't wipe microwaves, 100% => can wipe microwaves. Compared to "human performance" from a discretionary sample of representatives.


Submitting entries

Submission of entries can be done at github.com/michaelb/laundrybench. Data are carefully reviewed by human-level intelligence agents before inclusion into the general dataset, upon presentation of the benchmark's results, verified by a third-party.

Note: This website is fully hand-coded to avoid AIs boosting their own scores, because this is a serious benchmark project.
